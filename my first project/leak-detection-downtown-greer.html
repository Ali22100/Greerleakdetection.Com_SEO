Seasonal Leak Detection Considerations in Downtown Greer

Downtown Greer's climate affects when leaks occur and how they manifest. Here's what to expect each season.

Spring rains in Downtown Greer can saturate the soil, increasing pressure on underground pipes.

What to expect
This is when we see more underground leaks and slab leaks. The fluctuating temperatures can also cause pipes to expand and contract.
Good habit
Check your crawl space or basement for signs of moisture after heavy rains. If you notice a musty smell, call us for an inspection.
When to call
When something changes and you are not sure why. A quick call settles it.

Summer heat can dry out the soil, causing it to shift and crack pipes.

What to expect
Pool leaks are also more common as pools are in heavy use. Water bills often spike in summer, which can reveal hidden leaks.
Good habit
Monitor your water bill closely. If it jumps unexpectedly, schedule a leak detection before the problem worsens.
When to call
When something changes and you are not sure why. A quick call settles it.

Fall is a good time to prepare for winter.

What to expect
As temperatures drop, pipes can become more brittle. We often find leaks that started in summer but weren't noticed until fall.
Good habit
Have your plumbing inspected before the first freeze. Insulate exposed pipes in crawl spaces and attics.
When to call
When something changes and you are not sure why. A quick call settles it.

Freezing temperatures can cause pipes to burst, especially in older homes with poor insulation.

What to expect
Downtown Greer sees occasional hard freezes, and we get emergency calls for burst pipes.
