What our Underground Leak Detection service includes

Every detection call includes a full survey, instrument scan, leak confirmation, and a written summary of findings.

Work order

Underground Leak Detection
  • Full property survey: We walk the property, shut off fixtures, and watch the meter to confirm a live leak. We map the pipe route from the meter to the house and note any irrigation or pool lines that could be the source.
  • Acoustic and thermal scan: We pressurize the line and sweep it with an acoustic microphone and thermal imaging camera. This narrows the leak to a 12- to 18-inch zone, which keeps excavation small and your landscaping intact.
  • Leak confirmation and marking: A pipe inspection camera or tracer gas detector confirms the exact break. We mark the spot with paint and flagging and show you the readings so you see the evidence before we quote any repair.
  • Written findings summary: You get a written record of the line material, depth, leak location, and repair recommendation. That document is useful for insurance, resale, or the next plumber who works on the system.
  • Repair and pressure retest: If you approve the repair, we excavate, replace the failed section with new pipe and fittings, and re-pressurize the line with a pressure testing gauge. We hold the pressure to confirm zero drop before we backfill.

Tools and methods we use to find leaks

Each method answers a different question. Together they leave very little to guess.

Listens for the hiss of pressurized water escaping a pipe. We sweep the line and narrow the leak to a 12- to 18-inch zone. Sewerin and Gutermann units are our go-to for underground work.

Shows temperature differences where water is pooling under soil or concrete. It is the fastest way to map a slab leak or a yard leak before we confirm with acoustics.

Pressurizes the line and holds it to confirm there is a live leak and, after repair, that the leak has stopped. We use Fluke and RIDGID gauges on every job.

Injects a safe gas mixture into the line and sniffs for it at the surface. It is the most precise method for hard-to-find leaks under driveways or patios.

Runs inside the pipe to show the exact break, corrosion, or root intrusion. It confirms what the acoustic and thermal readings suggest before we dig.
