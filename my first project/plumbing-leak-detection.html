What Is Included in Our Plumbing Leak Detection Service

Work order

Plumbing Leak Detection
  • Full Pressure Test: We isolate the supply and drain systems and use a pressure testing gauge to confirm whether the leak is on the pressurized side or the drain side. This step alone saves hours of unnecessary searching.
  • Acoustic and Thermal Scan: We walk the property with an acoustic leak detection microphone and a thermal imaging camera. The microphone hears the leak through concrete, and the camera shows temperature changes from moisture. Both are included at no extra charge.
  • Moisture Mapping: A moisture meter tells us how far water has spread from the leak. We map the affected area so you know whether drywall, insulation, or flooring needs to be dried or replaced after the repair.
  • Marked Location and Photos: We mark the confirmed leak location with paint or tape and photograph it. You get a clear visual record of where the problem is, which is useful for insurance claims or for a second opinion.
  • Written Findings Summary: Before we leave, we give you a written summary of what we tested, what we found, and what repair options make sense. If we can fix it same day, we will quote that on the spot.

How Our Leak Detection Process Works

Every job follows the same five steps.

  1. Phone Intake

    We ask what you are seeing: a high water bill, a wet spot, a sound of running water, or a warm floor.

  2. System Isolation

    On arrival we shut off fixtures and appliances one at a time, then watch the pressure testing gauge.

  3. Acoustic and Thermal Scan

    We walk the house with an acoustic leak detection microphone and a thermal imaging camera. The microphone picks up the hiss of pressurized water through concrete or drywall.

  4. Confirm and Mark

  5. Repair Handoff

Tools and Methods We Use for Leak Detection

    A pool leak can waste thousands of gallons a month, damage the pump, and soften the ground around the deck. It often gets mistaken for evaporation.
