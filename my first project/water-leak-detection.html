What's Included in Our Water Leak Detection Service

Every detection job includes a full system check, not just the spot you called about. Here's what you get when we show up.

Work order

Water Leak Detection
  • Full System Pressure Test: We isolate the supply system and pressurize it with a pressure testing gauge. If it won't hold, we know there's an active leak. This test takes 15 to 20 minutes and tells us whether the problem is on the supply side or the drain side.
  • Acoustic and Thermal Sweep: We walk the property with an acoustic leak detection microphone and thermal imaging camera. This covers visible pipe runs, slab areas, and any spots you've flagged. We're listening for the hiss of escaping water and looking for temperature differences.
  • Moisture Mapping: A moisture meter gives us readings across the affected area. We map how far the water has spread so you know whether mold from hidden leaks is a risk. You get a written record of the readings before and after repair.
  • Written Findings and Photos: You get a summary of what we found, where it is, and what it will take to fix. Photos of the thermal images and moisture readings are included. This documentation is useful for insurance claims and for tracking the repair.
  • Repair Quote On the Spot: Once we've located the leak, we quote the repair before we start. You decide whether to proceed. If you approve, we often complete the repair the same visit using materials already on the truck.

How Our Leak Detection Process Works

Every job follows the same five steps. You know what's happening and what it costs before we start cutting.

  1. Phone Triage

    You describe the symptoms: high water bill, warm floor spot, running meter with everything off, or a damp baseboard. We ask a few questions to narrow the likely source.

  2. On-Site Inspection

    A technician walks the property with a moisture meter and thermal imaging camera. We check the water meter, shut-off valves, visible pipe runs, and any areas you've flagged.

  3. Acoustic and Pressure Testing

    We pressurize the system with a pressure testing gauge and listen through an acoustic leak detection microphone. For slab leaks, we use a pipe inspection camera to check pipe condition.

  4. Repair and Verification

    Once located, we repair the line using copper pipe, PEX tubing, or epoxy resin depending on the pipe material and access.

  5. Documentation and Next Steps

    We leave you with a written summary: what we found, what we fixed, and what to watch.

Tools and Methods We Use for Leak Detection

Each method answers a different question. Together they leave very little to guess.

Detects temperature differences caused by hot water leaks under slabs or behind walls. Fluke and RIDGID models give us a clear picture of where the heat is coming from.

Confirms whether the supply system holds pressure. If it drops, we know there's an active leak. This is the first test we run on every job.
